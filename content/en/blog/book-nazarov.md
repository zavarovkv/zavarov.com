## A little history

Karl Ludwig von Bertalanffy was a pioneer of systems thinking. He published his major work on the subject in 1968.

## A little terminology

A system is a set of interconnected elements that forms a coherent whole. The term “system” describes something large and complex that is not fully understood but is nevertheless a whole. Unlike a “set,” the concept of a system implies and emphasizes coherence and regularity.

## Properties of a system

1. A system has **boundaries** and is defined within them.
2. There are **connections** between its elements that determine their interdependence.
3. **Emergence** means that the system has properties its individual elements do not possess. Its functionality exceeds the sum of their individual functionalities.
4. **Hierarchy** means each element can be considered a separate system, while the system itself can be considered an element of another system.

**Systems thinking** is a way of looking at the world, distinguishing objects within it and finding connections between them.

## Types of systems

- A **goal-directed system** has its goal assigned from outside.
- A **purposeful system** sets its own goal and determines how to achieve it.
- A **social system**.
