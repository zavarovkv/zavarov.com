Most contemporary philosophers are historians of philosophy. In the past, philosophy was inseparable from science; since the twentieth century, it has been inseparable from art. In Kosuth's own words: “The twentieth century may be called the end of philosophy and the beginning of art.”

Aesthetics and art are not the same thing. These concepts need to be distinguished.

## The function of art

- More than half of the best work created in the preceding few years (written in 1965) belongs to neither painting nor sculpture. That is a plausible explanation for art's diminishing role in contemporary life.
- Marcel Duchamp was the first to raise the question of art's function, giving art its identity.
- The shift from “appearance” to “conception” marked the beginning of “modern art.”
- Art exists only conceptually.
- Aesthetics is indifferent to art.
- Assessing and understanding contemporary art requires prior knowledge of the concept of art and the particular artist's concepts.
- Art exists only for itself. Art is the only claim art makes. Art is the definition of art.
