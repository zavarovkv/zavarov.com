Here are some writers I enjoy reading on product management and related topics. I often return to their articles, especially when I'm looking for creative inspiration.

- [Marty Cagan](https://www.svpg.com/team/marty-cagan/) — an iconic figure in the product world and author of the widely popular book series: Inspired, Empowered, and Transformed. Founder of SVPG and an active blogger on product management. His ideas about product discovery and empowered teams have become industry standards.
- [Paul Graham](https://www.paulgraham.com/articles.html) — co-founder of Y Combinator and author of essays that stand the test of time. He writes about startups, thinking, and decision-making — concisely, precisely, and without filler.
- [Andrew Chen](https://andrewchen.com/) — partner at Andreessen Horowitz and author of a popular blog on growth marketing.
- [Vitalik Buterin](https://vitalik.eth.limo/) — founder of Ethereum and an active blogger. He writes deep technical and philosophical essays on decentralization and the future of technology.
- [Grigory Bakunov](https://t.me/addmeto) — founder of radio-t, who previously led technology outreach at Yandex. Writes about technology, management, and life in tech — sharp and free of corporate speak.
- [Evgeny Chereshnev](https://blog.cheresh.me/) — author of Life Form #4. His blog covers artificial intelligence, technology, and world events.
- [Oleg Yakubenkov](https://gopractice.ru/) — founder of GoPractice and author of the #1 most popular blog on product analytics. His simulators and articles are a must-read for anyone who wants to get serious about metrics and unit economics.
- [Artem Gorbunov & Co.](https://bureau.ru/soviet/) — practical advice from leading practitioners. Short and to the point — covering writing, interfaces, negotiation, and management.
- [Melissa Perri](https://melissaperri.com/blog/) — author of Escaping the Build Trap. Writes about creating value through products, product culture, and why companies get stuck in feature-factory mode.
- [Teresa Torres](https://www.producttalk.org/articles/) — author of Continuous Discovery Habits. Her blog covers discovery practices: opportunity solution trees, customer interviews, and the habits of strong product teams.
- [Wes Kao](https://www.weskao.com/) — runs an excellent practical blog on management, product thinking, and career development. Publishes consistently.
- [Ben Thompson](https://stratechery.com/) — tech analyst and author of the Stratechery blog. Offers deep dives into the business strategies of technology companies, platform models, and competitive market dynamics.
- [Valeria Rozova](https://library.wannabe.ru/) — product leader and curator of an article library on product topics, written by graduates of her courses. The articles are highly practical and detailed — a great resource for going deep on specific product skills.
