Product managers present constantly: they finish a research study → share the findings; a new quarter kicks off → announce goals; a quarter, half-year, or year wraps up → review results; and so on.

Data visualization tends to be the weak spot in these presentations, so I put together a Google Sheets template where I'll collect and update *reference* chart examples for the most common product, business, and financial data.

<a class="button" target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1y4PhhsZyB9i9ZLPvEZW4tmZXXhODPhuAlUbg9QIFy7Q/edit#gid=2114637130">Download the template</a>

The document includes 5 templates:

## 1. Market Size Visualization Chart

A bar chart showing revenue for the past 5 years alongside a line chart tracking year-over-year growth rates. The simplest and most intuitive way to visualize market size over time.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-market.png" alt="Market size visualization chart">

## 2. Top Competitors Visualization Chart

A donut chart showing each key competitor's share of revenue.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-competitors.webp" alt="Top competitors visualization chart">

## 3. Year-over-Year Revenue Growth Chart

A visualization that shows not just annual revenue, but also quarterly breakdowns — along with year-over-year revenue changes.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-revenue.png" alt="Year-over-year revenue growth chart">

## 4. Simple P&L Visualization Chart

A combo chart where positive bars represent Revenue and negative bars represent Expenses, with a dotted line showing Profit. Clean and easy to read.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-pnl-simple.webp" alt="Simple P&L visualization chart">

## 5. Detailed P&L Visualization Chart

Unlike the previous template, this one breaks Expenses down by category, with each category's share of total expenses shown explicitly. Less visually clean than the simple version, but more granular.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-pnl-detailed.webp" alt="Detailed P&L visualization chart">
