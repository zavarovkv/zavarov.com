Product managers present constantly: they run research → share findings; a new quarter kicks off → they announce goals; a quarter, half-year, or year wraps up → they review results; and so on.

Data visualization tends to be a weak spot in these presentations, so I decided to put together a Google Sheets template where I'll collect and update *reference* chart examples covering the most common product, business, and financial data scenarios.

<a class="button" target="_blank" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1y4PhhsZyB9i9ZLPvEZW4tmZXXhODPhuAlUbg9QIFy7Q/edit#gid=2114637130">Download template</a>

The document contains 5 templates:

## 1. Market Size Visualization Chart

A bar chart showing revenue for the past 5 years broken down by year, paired with a line chart showing year-over-year growth rates. The simplest and most intuitive way to visualize market size over time.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-market.png" alt="Market size visualization chart">

## 2. Top Competitors Visualization Chart

A donut chart showing each key competitor's share of revenue.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-competitors.webp" alt="Top competitors visualization chart">

## 3. Year-over-Year Revenue Growth Chart

A visualization that shows not only annual revenue, but also quarterly figures alongside year-over-year revenue changes.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-revenue.png" alt="Year-over-year revenue growth chart">

## 4. Simple P&L Visualization Chart

A combo chart where positive bars represent Revenue and negative bars represent Expenses. A dashed line shows Profit. Clean and easy to read.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-pnl-simple.webp" alt="Simple P&L visualization chart">

## 5. Detailed P&L Visualization Chart

Unlike the previous template, this one includes a breakdown of Expenses by category, with each category's share of total expenses shown. Less visually clean than the previous option, but more granular.

<img loading="lazy" style="max-width: 512px" src="/images/diagrams-pnl-detailed.webp" alt="Detailed P&L visualization chart">
