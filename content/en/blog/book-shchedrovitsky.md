The book is based on recordings of Georgy Schedrovitsky's lecture course for managers of large Soviet enterprises.

I found his reflections on management as a system and on treating management as an object of study valuable. But keeping up with every turn of his thinking was difficult, sometimes impossible. That desire to understand more deeply leads me to further reading on systems analysis, systems theory and systems thinking.

For example, these books joined my required-reading list:

- Теория систем и системный анализ, V. N. Chernyshov and A. V. Chernyshov.
- General Systems Theory: Mathematical Foundations, M. D. Mesarovic and Y. Takahara.

The book could be taken apart into quotations and notes to return to. The simplest, most memorable quotation is:

> “However many management books you read, that will not make you a manager.”

For anyone interested in Schedrovitsky's other work, the most complete bibliography can be found on the website of the scientific foundation named after G. P. Schedrovitsky.

If you feel like rereading the book, I would recommend returning to the following work after some time. Much of the content is the same, but the structure differs:

- Путеводитель по основным понятиям и схемам методологии организации, руководства и управления, based on the work of G. P. Schedrovitsky.
