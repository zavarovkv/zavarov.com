A Google Sheets template for prioritizing product initiatives using three methods: ICE, RICE, and DRICE. For a deeper dive into the methods, see the article [«ICE, RICE, DRICE Prioritization Methods»](/drice/).

## What's inside

**ICE** is the simplest prioritization method. Each initiative is scored on three parameters from 0 to 10:

- Impact (effect on the metric)
- Confidence (confidence in the estimate)
- Ease (ease of implementation)
- ICE Score = I × C × E

**RICE** is a more precise method developed by Intercom. The spreadsheet includes columns for:

- Reach (number of users reached)
- Impact (effect on the metric, scale 0.25–3)
- Confidence (confidence in the estimate, 20%–100%)
- Effort (effort required)
- RICE Score = (R × I × C) / E

The higher the score, the higher the priority of the initiative. The template includes filled-in examples for reference.
