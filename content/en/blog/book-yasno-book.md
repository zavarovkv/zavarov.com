Unlike “Пиши, сокращай,” this book works not only as a desk reference but also as something to read from cover to cover. That is what I did, with great interest.

Retelling its contents would be pointless, because understanding comes from working through the many examples of what to do and what to avoid. The book follows the same rules and principles Ilyahov teaches:

> “A thesis can be written in a minute, but the examples took years to collect. That is why all the summaries, reviews and shortened versions of ‘Пиши, сокращай’ fail. The moment you reduce this book to its main points, it loses its power: without the examples, those points cannot be deeply understood or applied.”

It should interest anyone who writes in public, even if only for a blog or a Telegram channel, and wants to start applying Ilyahov's advice to their own work immediately.
