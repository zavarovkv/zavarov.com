I want to share the services and apps I use on a regular basis to get things done at work. Maybe you'll discover something new and make your work a little more enjoyable.

## Product Awareness and UX

- [ProductHunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) — the go-to resource for staying on top of product trends, keeping up with new product launches and the evolution of existing ones.
- [UserOnboard](https://www.useronboard.com/) — a library of detailed UX teardowns of popular products like Gmail, Instagram, Apple Music, Netflix, and more.

## Research and A/B Testing

- [Statista](https://www.statista.com/) — a knowledge base with up-to-date market analytics and research. A corporate account unlocks detailed reports, but the free version still gives you access to the basics.
- [Yandex Wordstat](https://wordstat.yandex.com/) — helps you analyze user search queries in the Russian-language internet segment.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) — a massive library of academic papers on virtually any topic.
- [Evan's Awesome A/B Tools](https://www.evanmiller.org/ab-testing/) — one of the oldest and most popular online tools for planning and analyzing A/B tests: sample size estimation and experiment result evaluation.
- [GoodUI](https://goodui.org/) — a pattern library featuring A/B tests and breakdowns of their results.

## Diagrams, Flowcharts, and Graphics

- [Miro](https://miro.com) — the de facto standard for visual collaboration.
- [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) — a tool for generating diagrams from text descriptions (code). Integrates with a wide range of modern wiki systems: Notion, Google Docs, Confluence.
- [Draw.io](https://www.drawio.com/) — a simple and convenient tool for creating more complex diagrams than Mermaid supports. Saves files directly to Google Drive and requires no registration or login.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) — the best tool for creating visual assets; the free version is more than enough for most everyday tasks.
- [Emojipedia](https://emojipedia.org/) — an emoji reference for adding a touch of personality to requirements docs and task descriptions.

## Personal and Team Productivity

- [Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/) — a clean, beautifully designed to-do app for Mac and iOS with minimalist aesthetics and great typography, perfect for design perfectionists.
- [Ilya Birman's Typography Layout](https://ilyabirman.ru/typography-layout/) — a keyboard layout that makes it easy to type proper typographic characters quickly (✓≈≠₽×↓−""). If you write a lot and care about how your text looks, give it a try — it's a joy to use.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) — an app for organizing notes in Markdown (.md) format.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) — my replacement for Google Search.
- [Google Sheets, Slides, Colab](https://google.com) — the baseline toolkit for any manager.

## Rapid MVP Development and Vibe Coding

- [Cursor IDE](https://cursor.com/) — a supercharged code editor that lets you build apps and services with minimal programming knowledge, incredibly fast.
- [GitHub / GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages) — deploy static sites to production quickly and for free.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) — deploy more complex services to production quickly and for free.
