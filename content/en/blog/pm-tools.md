I want to share the services and tools I use on a daily basis to get work done. Maybe you'll discover something new and make your workflow a little more comfortable.

<h2>Product Awareness and UX</h2>

- [ProductHunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) — the go-to resource for staying on top of the product landscape: new launches, updates, and the evolution of existing products.
- [UserOnboard](https://www.useronboard.com/) — a library of detailed UX teardowns for popular services like Gmail, Instagram, Apple Music, Netflix, and more.

<h2>Research and A/B Testing</h2>

- [Statista](https://www.statista.com/) — a knowledge base with up-to-date market analytics and research. A corporate account with access to detailed reports is great to have, but the free version still lets you view the basics.
- [Yandex Wordstat](https://wordstat.yandex.com/) — useful for analyzing user search queries in the Russian-language internet segment.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) — a massive library of academic papers on virtually any topic.
- [Evan's Awesome A/B Tools](https://www.evanmiller.org/ab-testing/) — one of the oldest and most popular online tools for planning and analyzing A/B tests: sample size estimation and experiment result evaluation.
- [GoodUI](https://goodui.org/) — a pattern library featuring A/B tests and breakdowns of their results.

<h2>Diagrams, Flowcharts, and Graphics</h2>

- [Miro](https://miro.com) — the de facto standard for visual collaboration.
- [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) — a tool for generating diagrams from plain text (code). Integrates with many modern wiki systems: Notion, Google Docs, Confluence.
- [Draw.io](https://www.drawio.com/) — a simple, user-friendly tool for creating more complex diagrams than Mermaid supports. Saves files directly to Google Drive and requires no registration or login.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) — the best tool for creating visual assets; the free version is more than enough for most straightforward tasks.
- [Emojipedia](https://emojipedia.org/) — an emoji reference for adding a touch of personality to requirements and task descriptions.

<h2>Personal and Team Productivity</h2>

- [Things](https://culturedcode.com/things/) — a clean, beautifully designed to-do app for Mac and iOS with minimalist aesthetics and excellent typography, perfect for design perfectionists.
- [Ilya Birman's Typography Layout](https://ilyabirman.ru/typography-layout/) — a keyboard layout that makes it easy to type proper typographic characters and symbols quickly (✓≈≠₽x↓−""). If you write a lot and care about how your text looks, give Birman's layout a try — it's a joy to use.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) — a note-taking app built around Markdown (.md) files.
- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) — a replacement for Google Search.
- [Google Sheets, Slides, Colab](https://google.com) — the essential baseline toolkit for any product manager.

<h2>Rapid MVP Development and Vibe Coding</h2>

- [Cursor IDE](https://cursor.com/) — a code editor on steroids that lets you build apps and services with minimal programming knowledge, incredibly fast.
- [GitHub / GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages) — deploy static sites to production quickly and for free.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) — deploy more complex services to production quickly and for free.
