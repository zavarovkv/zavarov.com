The book that made me put Maugham aside for a while.

## Quotations to remember

> “The course of your lives is known in advance. You will grow up, but before you get old, before you even reach middle age, your internal organs will start being taken for transplants. You were brought into the world for these donations. You were made differently from the actors in the films on your videotapes, differently even from me. You are being raised for a particular purpose, and your fate is already known. So there must be no more talk like that. Before long you will leave Hailsham, and the day of the first donation is not very far away for any of you. Remember that.”

> “We all die. None of us truly perceives life or feels it all the time.”

> “You are always in a hurry, and even when you are not, you are too exhausted for a proper conversation. Soon the long working hours, the journeys and the snatches of sleep seep into you, become part of you, and anyone can see it in your posture, your eyes and the way you walk and talk. I do not mean that none of this has affected me. I have simply learned to live with it.”

> “The memories I value most remain as vivid as they ever were.”

> “We took your work because we hoped it would reveal your souls. Or, to put it more precisely, prove that you had souls.”
