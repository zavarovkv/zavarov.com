In Crossing the Chasm, Geoffrey Moore describes a strategy for developing a technology business around a very narrowly defined market. Choose one niche, fill it with your product, and only then move on to the next. Stay focused and work through the markets one at a time. That is how you successfully cross the chasm between visionaries and pragmatists.

I found it useful because most projects I work with have no concept of a niche marketing strategy at all. A must-read for anyone involved in internet marketing, especially startup founders.
