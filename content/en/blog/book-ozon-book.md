A good introduction to the complicated history of an internet business in a complicated Russia. It covers the difficulties involved in creating the country's first e-commerce brand (am I exaggerating a little? :)). Ozon's history has been long and far from straightforward…

While reading, I often thought of Branson, his gripping stories and his undeniable good fortune. I did not see much luck in Ozon's story. The business was built from scratch, with many factors working against it.

Ozon kept fighting and investing as much as possible in growth and development. It came close to financial collapse but managed to withstand every difficult situation. The company's foundations are solid. The book often compares it to Amazon, and perhaps at certain points there were similarities. By now, however, the companies have clearly chosen different markets for development. Even the name, it turns out, was chosen by analogy.

This post will have no conclusion, because Ozon's story continues and apparently still has major changes ahead…
