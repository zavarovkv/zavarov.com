> The weapon used to end one war (the reference is to nuclear weapons) was now being used to start another.

> The true purpose of waging war?
>
> To keep industry working at full capacity while maintaining all its mechanisms and a low standard of living. A well-fed, physically comfortable citizen with a wide choice of goods and the money to buy them is a poor subject for an oligarchic state. Someone whose stomach is full of meat has little interest in the bare bones of political doctrine. The poor show the greatest readiness for fanatical loyalty to the ruling party. Moreover, loyalty and what used to be called patriotism are much easier to sustain when the enemy appears to be at the gates.

> The Party has come to power, but the sewer is still blocked. The incompatibility of real life with the abstractions of Party doctrine was what filled Orwell with despair.
