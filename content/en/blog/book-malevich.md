- There is nothing in the world that remains the same, and therefore there is no eternal beauty.
- The point is neither color nor form nor their interdependence. Rather, the essence of painting is the artist's connection with the world through sensation.
- Color and form do not shape anything. They merely seek to express the hidden force of sensations.
- When Cubism and Futurism appeared, audiences and critics said there was nothing in the paintings. They meant there were no signs of objects, no recognizable forms. The painterly relationships that created new forms said nothing to society, because most people could not perceive painting outside the forms of nature.
- Science and art have no boundaries, because what can be known is boundless and innumerable; innumerability and boundlessness are equal to zero.
- You put birds in cages for your pleasure too.
- I transformed myself in the zero of form and went beyond zero into creation: into Suprematism, into a new painterly realism, into non-objective creation.
- The efforts of artistic authorities to steer art along the path of common sense have produced zero creativity.
- All this confirms that your body lives in the present while your soul wears your grandmother's old bodice.
- Why do you not wear your grandmothers' clothes when you swoon over their powdered portraits?
- A painted face offers a pitiful parody of life, and that hint is merely a reminder of the living.
- Everything runs from the past toward the future, but everything must live in the present, because in the future the apple trees will have finished flowering.
- An artist must now know what happens in a painting and why.
- Once, the artist lived by a mood: waiting for moonrise or dusk, putting green shades on lamps. All this tuned the artist like a violin.
- Yet when asked why the face was crooked or green, the artist could not give a precise answer.
- “That is what I want. That is how I like it…”
- Yesterday, with our heads held high, we defended Futurism. Now we spit on it with pride.
- Academic realists are the last descendants of the savage.
- However much we rearrange furniture in rooms, we neither enlarge the rooms nor create a new form for them.
- There is a great difference between the art of creating and the art of repeating.
- All the Renaissance masters achieved great results in anatomy.
- But they did not achieve a truthful impression of the body. Their paintings do not convey bodies, nor do their landscapes convey living light, despite the blue veins showing through their figures' flesh.
- Only dull and powerless artists hide their art behind sincerity.
- Art needs truth, not sincerity.
