Some books belong on your desk. Getting Real is one of them, whatever role I happen to be in: developer, manager, or director of something or other :)

I will not quote it, because the whole book distills the experience of the 37signals team, with plenty of overlap with agile methodology. I would strongly recommend it to anyone interested in time management, or simply anyone who understands the value of time.
