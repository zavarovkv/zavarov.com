**For those just starting out** I'll help you understand how the product manager role works, what managers expect, and what core competencies matter. After the session, you'll know what's missing for a confident start and how to build your own growth plan. I can help you review or put together your resume and prepare for case interviews to land the job you want.

**For experienced product managers** I'll help you assess your current level and build a personalized development plan. We'll go through the key competencies, identify your growth areas, and map out concrete steps toward your next career milestone. I can also help with questions around organizing the product process and defining the right product metrics.

You're welcome to bring any practical question — we'll dig into it, find a solution, and put together a clear action plan.

Each session lasts an hour. We can meet online or in person in Moscow. After the meeting, you'll receive a detailed action plan tailored to your topic.
