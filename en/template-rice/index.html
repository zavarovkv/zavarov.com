ICE, RICE, DRICE Prioritization Framework Template

A Google Sheets template for prioritizing product initiatives using three frameworks: ICE, RICE, and DRICE. For a deeper dive into the methods, check out the article «ICE, RICE, DRICE Prioritization Methods».

Download template

What’s inside#

ICE — the simplest prioritization method. Each initiative is scored on three factors from 0 to 10:

  • Impact (effect on the metric)
  • Confidence (confidence in the estimate)
  • Ease (ease of implementation)
  • ICE Score = I × C × E

RICE — a more precise method developed by Intercom. The table includes the following columns:

  • Reach (number of users affected)
  • Impact (effect on the metric, scale 0.25–3)
  • Confidence (confidence in the estimate, 20%–100%)
  • Effort (work required)
  • RICE Score = (R × I × C) / E

The higher the score, the higher the priority of the initiative. The template includes sample entries to help illustrate how it works.
