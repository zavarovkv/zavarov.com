How the team works

  1. 1

    Qui‑Gon makes a plan

    He discusses the task in Telegram and asks follow-up questions there. He defines the expected outcome, chooses the specialists, and makes a plan.

  2. 2

    The agents do the work

    Each agent has its own memory and specialized skills. They handle assigned work and can also identify and implement project improvements on their own, without waiting for a new task.

  3. 3

    Qui‑Gon reviews and presents the result

    He checks the results against the original brief and requests revisions when needed. He brings the specialists' work together and presents the outcome.
